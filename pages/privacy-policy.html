Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026 • Effective Date: September 1, 2026

At TechPulse Trends ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible via https://www.techpulsetrends.com, protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our foundational commitments. This Privacy Policy document describes the types of personal information collected and recorded by TechPulse Trends and how we use and safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect

a) Automatically Collected Technical Information: When you navigate our website, our web servers and content delivery networks automatically log standard browser headers, including Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and approximate geographic region. This data is non-personally identifiable and is analyzed solely for infrastructure monitoring and Core Web Vitals optimization.

b) Voluntarily Provided Information: When you contact us via our contact form or subscribe to editorial alerts, we collect your name, email address, and any correspondence you submit.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Vendors

Google is a third-party advertising vendor on TechPulse Trends. Google utilizes cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie and interest-based identifiers) to serve advertisements to our site visitors based on their visit to this website and other websites across the Internet.

  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area Visitors)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • The right to access, update, or erase the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to restrict or object to our processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@techpulsetrends.com.

4. CCPA / CPRA Compliance (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal information collected, request deletion, and request that their personal data not be sold or shared. TechPulse Trends does not sell personal consumer data.

5. Children's Information (COPPA)

TechPulse Trends is targeted toward software engineering professionals and does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@techpulsetrends.com.
