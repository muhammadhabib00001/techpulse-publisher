Editorial Guidelines & Fact-Checking Policy

Published: September 1, 2026 • Compliant with Google Search EEAT Quality Rater Standards

1. Our Editorial Philosophy

TechPulse Trends is committed to publishing trustworthy, technically rigorous, and reproducible technology journalism. Every article undergoes structured editorial peer review before publication.

2. Fact-Checking & Code Verification Protocol

Our fact-checking protocol involves three distinct review tiers:

  • Primary Source Verification: All benchmark numbers, algorithmic specifications, and CVE security advisories are verified against primary engineering documentation, IETF RFCs, or academic preprints.
  • Code Reproduction: All published code samples and configuration blocks are tested in isolated environments.
  • Peer Architecture Review: Articles are reviewed by a secondary domain architect prior to final publication.

3. Generative AI Use & Disclosure Policy

We believe in transparency regarding the use of AI tools. While our authors may use AI utilities for initial grammar polishing or formatting assistance, all conceptual analyses, architecture blueprints, synthesis, and technical conclusions are independently conceived and validated by human experts. We strictly prohibit the mass generation of unedited, hallucinated AI content.

4. Corrections Policy

If an error is discovered in an article, we publish a clear correction notice with the update timestamp. Readers can submit corrections to editorial@techpulsetrends.com.
