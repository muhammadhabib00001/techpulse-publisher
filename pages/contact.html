Contact GenAlphaMagazines Newsroom

1. Submit a News Tip or Story Idea

Do you have a breaking news tip, an investigative lead on municipal governance, or a heartwarming community achievement we should cover? GenAlphaMagazines actively welcomes news submissions from community residents, civic leaders, non-profits, and business owners.

When submitting a tip, please provide as much context as possible, including relevant dates, locations, public records, and contact information for key participants. Confidentiality can be requested upon submission.
