Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how TechPulse Trends uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function efficiently and provide analytical data to website owners.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies & LocalStorage: Required for site navigation, theme preferences (Dark/Light mode persistence), and storing your cookie consent status.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Measure aggregate page load times and Core Web Vitals telemetry to ensure optimal performance across devices.
  • Advertising & AdSense Cookies: Used by Google AdSense and its partners to serve relevant advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.

3. How Can You Control Cookies?

You have the right to accept or decline optional cookies using our on-page cookie consent banner. You can also adjust your browser settings to delete or block cookies. For more details on managing ad preferences, visit Google Ads Settings.
