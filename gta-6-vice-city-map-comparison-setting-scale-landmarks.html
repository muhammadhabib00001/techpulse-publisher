GTA 6 Vice City Map Comparison: Setting Scale, Landmarks

MR
Marcus Reid
Senior Tech and Gaming Correspondent
Published: Sep 8, 2026

GTA 6 Vice City Map Scale and Setting Overview

Quick Answer: The GTA 6 map centered around Leonida and Vice City is estimated to be roughly 2.5 times larger than Los Santos from GTA 5, costing Rockstar an estimated $2 billion in total development budget. Based on leaked mapping projects and official trailer analysis, the open world successfully blends dense urban high rises from Miami with massive rural swamps inspired by the Everglades.

When Rockstar Games officially revealed the return to Vice City in the first trailer, open world enthusiasts immediately began analyzing the geographical scale compared to previous franchise entries. The shift from the fictionalized West Coast of Los Santos back to the vibrant neon soaked streets of Vice City and the broader state of Leonida represents a monumental leap in hardware utilization. Players purchasing the game at the standard retail price of $69.99 will experience a digital recreation of Florida that completely redefines open world density, interior accessibility, and regional diversity.

Understanding the actual scale of the GTA 6 map requires looking at historical franchise progression. While GTA San Andreas offered three distinct cities with vast empty countryside between them, and GTA 5 focused on one massive urban center surrounded by desert mountains, GTA 6 takes a hybrid approach. The developers have packed both extreme urban verticality and sprawling, ecosystem rich rural sectors into a single seamless landmass. This architectural shift changes how players navigate missions, engage in police chases, and explore the economy of Leonida.

Comparing Map Scale: GTA 6 Versus GTA 5 and San Andreas

Measuring Square Kilometers Against Los Santos

Fan community mapping projects derived from leaked development footage estimate the landmass of Leonida to span approximately 125 to 135 square kilometers of playable terrain. In direct comparison, the Los Santos map in GTA 5 covers roughly 75 square kilometers including water bodies. This means players are getting a roughly 70 percent increase in total land area, with a vastly higher percentage dedicated to interior spaces, high rise buildings, and commercially dense districts.

Waterways and Ocean Exploration

Unlike previous titles where the ocean served mostly as an invisible boundary or a shallow swimming zone, Vice City features expansive coastal waters, barrier islands, and complex river systems. The inclusion of the Keys inspired island chain adds dozens of miles of drivable causeways, bridges, and shallow diving zones. This aquatic focus allows for sophisticated boat physics, marina economies, and underwater exploration that mirrors real world South Florida boating culture.

Urban Density Versus Rural Wilderness

The distribution of space in Leonida is heavily weighted toward activity. Instead of wide stretches of empty desert, the map utilizes dense suburbs, trailer parks, industrial ports, and massive swamp networks. Every square mile features dynamic pedestrian AI routines, wildlife populations inspired by the Everglades, and intricate highway systems that replicate the Florida Turnpike and I 95.

Classic Vice City Landmarks Reimagined for Modern Hardware

Ocean Drive and the Art Deco Strip

The legendary Ocean Drive from the 2002 classic title returns with breathtaking next generation fidelity. Neon signs cast dynamic reflections on wet asphalt during tropical downpours, and the pastel colored Art Deco hotels feature fully realized lobbies, accessible rooftops, and bustling tourist foot traffic. Iconic locations like the Ocean View Hotel are rendered with microscopic detail, honoring the original aesthetic while delivering modern architectural complexity.

Port Gellhorn and Industrial Zones

Replacing the older industrial dock areas, Port Gellhorn serves as a sprawling commercial hub on the western edge of the map. This working-class industrial sector provides stark contrast to the wealthy beachfront resorts of Vice City proper. Featuring container yards, massive crane systems, and dilapidated motels, this district anchors gritty criminal storylines and high speed vehicle pursuits reminiscent of classic action cinema.

The Leonida Everglades and Grassrivers

Swapping the Mount Chiliad mountain range for a sprawling subtropical wetland, the Grassrivers region introduces unprecedented ecosystem depth. Players will navigate airboats through murky mangrove swamps populated by alligators, wading birds, and remote fishing outposts. This wilderness area challenges standard driving techniques, forcing players to rely on specialized all terrain vehicles and watercraft.

The State of Leonida: Districts, Counties, and Towns

Vice City Metropolitan Area

The beating heart of the map is Vice City itself, divided into multiple distinct islands and mainland districts. From the affluent shopping promenades of Little Haiti and Vice Beach to the glittering nightlife districts, the urban core features unprecedented building accessibility. Players can enter convenience stores, pawn shops, diners, and multi story corporate offices during both freeroam and heist missions.

Grassrivers County and Suburbs

Surrounding the urban sprawl are rural farming communities, sleepy coastal towns, and sprawling trailer parks that capture the unique culture of rural Florida. These areas feature localized economies, distinct law enforcement responses, and unique side activities ranging from swamp buggy racing to alligator trapping. The contrast between wealthy yacht owners and impoverished inland residents forms a central theme of the game world.

Keys Island Chain and Southern Outposts

Stretching far to the south, the archipelago of islands offers a tropical paradise vibe complete with tiki bars, rental boat shacks, and long scenic bridges. Driving across these interconnected spans provides breathtaking ocean vistas, high stakes police roadblocks, and hidden contraband drops that reward thorough exploration of the southern maritime border.

Final Thoughts

Key Takeaway: The GTA 6 map represents a monumental evolution for open world design by marrying the vibrant aesthetic of classic Vice City with the breathtaking scale and density of modern hardware capabilities.

By expanding the landmass beyond previous franchise entries and introducing deep ecosystem diversity across urban streets and rural swamps, Rockstar Games is setting a new industry benchmark for immersive virtual environments that justify years of anticipation. Frequently Asked Questions

How much larger is the GTA 6 map compared to GTA 5?

Current community mapping estimates and insider reports suggest that the GTA 6 map located in Leonida will be roughly 2.5 times larger than the Los Santos map found in GTA 5, offering significantly more interior accessibility and urban density across all districts.

What real world location is the setting of GTA 6 based on?

The setting of GTA 6 is heavily inspired by the state of Florida, specifically featuring a fictionalized version called Leonida. It prominently highlights Miami as Vice City alongside vast wetland regions modeled after the Everglades and a string of southern islands resembling the Florida Keys.

Will classic landmarks from the original 2002 Vice City return?

Yes, iconic locations like Ocean Drive, the pastel art deco hotels, and various beachside strips return with massive graphical overhauls, modern architectural layouts, and fully realized interior spaces that players can explore during both missions and freeroam.

Are there rural wilderness areas to explore outside of Vice City?

Absolutely. Unlike GTA 5 which featured desert mountains, GTA 6 includes the Grassrivers region, a massive subtropical swamp network inspired by the Everglades populated by dynamic wildlife, rural towns, trailer parks, and swamp buggy racing circuits.

How will building interiors function in the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar Games has significantly increased building accessibility in GTA 6, allowing players to enter a vastly higher percentage of structures including convenience stores, pawn shops, restaurants, nightclubs, and multi story corporate offices compared to previous titles.
