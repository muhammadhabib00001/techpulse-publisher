Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard pipe size for a residential shower drain?

Most residential shower drains require a two inch waste pipe to handle high volume water flow efficiently without backing up.

Why do my bathroom drains produce a gurgling sound?

Gurgling sounds usually indicate a blocked vent pipe that is starving the drainage system of necessary atmospheric air pressure.

How often should I clean my bathroom sink and shower traps?

Clearing hair and debris from sink stoppers and shower strainers every month keeps wastewater flowing smoothly and prevents major clogs.

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaners regularly?

Chemical drain cleaners generate excessive heat and corrosive reactions that weaken pipe joints, making mechanical snaking a much safer alternative.

What slope is required for horizontal waste drainage pipes?

Building codes typically mandate a downward slope of one quarter inch per foot for horizontal drainage lines to ensure proper gravitational flow.
