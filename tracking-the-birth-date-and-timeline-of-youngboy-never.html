Tracking The Birth Date And Timeline Of YoungBoy Never

JV
Julia Vance
Culture & Entertainment Columnist
Published: Sep 23, 2026

Quick Answer: YoungBoy Never Broke Again was born on October 20, 1999, in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. His career timeline spans from releasing his debut mixtape 'Life Before Fame' in 2015 to securing multiple Billboard chart-topping albums and accumulating billions of streams globally.

When tracking the birth date and timeline of YoungBoy Never Broke Again, music enthusiasts and industry analysts alike observe a career marked by relentless productivity, immense cultural impact, and profound personal trials. Born Kentrell DeSean Gaulden, the artist entered the world on October 20, 1999, in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Growing up in a challenging environment shaped by poverty and a turbulent family background, Gaulden found solace in music, using a makeshift microphone bought at age fourteen to record his earliest tracks. His trajectory mirrors the hustle often seen among famous celebrities in USA who leverage raw digital distribution to bypass traditional gatekeepers.

Understanding the chronological evolution of modern hip-hop requires a close examination of how regional artists transform localized followings into global phenomena through sheer volume and emotional authenticity. YoungBoy's distinct vocal delivery, characterized by raw vulnerability and aggressive street narratives, immediately resonated with a generation navigating similar systemic obstacles. From his early teenage mixtapes uploaded to YouTube to securing multimillion-dollar recording contracts with Atlantic Records and later Motown, his timeline is a masterclass in digital-era audience building and sustained artistic output.

Early Life, Birth Details, and Initial Musical Steps

Baton Rouge Roots and Upbringing

Kentrell DeSean Gaulden was raised primarily by his maternal grandmother due to his father being sentenced to 55 years in prison. Growing up on the north side of Baton Rouge, Gaulden experienced a harsh childhood that heavily influenced his worldview and artistic output. He dropped out of ninth grade to focus entirely on music, a high-risk decision that ultimately catalyzed his professional journey. During these formative years, he immersed himself in local studio sessions, developing a fierce work ethic and an ear for melodic trap production that would soon define his signature sound.

First Mixtapes and Legal Turning Points

In 2015, operating under the moniker NBA YoungBoy (Never Broke Again), he released his debut mixtape titled Life Before Fame. This was quickly followed by a string of independent projects including Mind of a Menace and Before I Go: Reloaded. These early releases established a regional buzz across Louisiana and neighboring southern states. However, his burgeoning career faced a severe hurdle in late 2016 when he was arrested in Austin, Texas, in connection with an alleged drive-by shooting. This period of incarceration temporarily halted his momentum but paradoxically heightened his street credibility and underground anticipation.

Release and Breakthrough with '38 Baby'

Upon his release from jail in early 2017, YoungBoy dropped the mixtape 38 Baby, which became a breakout success. Named after a notorious Baton Rouge neighborhood and his gang affiliations, the project featured guest appearances from notable southern acts like Boosie Badazz and Kevin Gates. The accompanying music videos racked up tens of millions of views on YouTube, proving that his audience remained fiercely loyal despite his legal troubles. This momentum paved the way for nationwide visibility and caught the attention of major record labels looking to sign the next major voice in southern rap.

Mainstream Breakthrough and Chart Domination

The Rise of 'AI YoungBoy' and National Attention

The release of his 2017 mixtape AI YoungBoy marked his official entry onto the Billboard charts, debuting at number 24 on the Billboard 200. The project featured the platinum-certified single "Untouchable," which solidified his ability to blend aggressive street anthems with introspective, melodic hooks. Critics began to take notice of his relentless output and the deep emotional connection he shared with his fanbase. His style even began to subtly influence red carpet fashion trends among younger hip-hop artists who embraced his signature distressed denim, layered luxury streetwear, and iced-out jewelry aesthetics.

Studio Albums and Billboard Records

In January 2018, YoungBoy released his debut studio album, Until Death Call My Name, which peaked at number 7 on the Billboard 200 and included the multi-platinum hit "Outside Today." Despite facing ongoing legal restrictions and periods of house arrest, his productivity never waned. He systematically flooded streaming platforms with high-charting projects, including AI YoungBoy 2 (2019) and Top (2020), both of which debuted at number one on the Billboard 200. According to data from YoungBoy Never Broke Again, he achieved the remarkable feat of earning multiple consecutive chart-topping albums while still in his early twenties.

Prolific Streaming Era and Independent Distribution

Entering the 2020s, YoungBoy cemented his status as one of the most-streamed artists globally, frequently rivaling pop and mainstream hip-hop heavyweights despite rarely making traditional radio appearances or media runs. His 2021 album Sincerely, Kentrell outperformed Drake’s Certified Lover Boy for the number one spot on the Billboard 200 during its debut week, making him one of the few artists to achieve this milestone while incarcerated. Following the fulfillment of his Atlantic Records contract, he signed a lucrative partnership with Motown Records, continuing to release sprawling, twenty-plus track projects that consistently dominate streaming charts within hours of release.

Legacy, Cultural Influence, and Ongoing Evolution

Redefining Fan Engagement and Output Volume

YoungBoy Never Broke Again fundamentally altered how modern rap artists interact with their core audience and manage their release schedules. Traditional album cycles lasting two to three years were abandoned in favor of a constant stream of content, singles, music videos, and full-length mixtapes dropped several times a year. This strategy starved the digital landscape of empty space, ensuring his name remained a constant trending topic across social media platforms like Twitter, TikTok, and YouTube. His raw, unfiltered approach established a new benchmark for authenticity in contemporary urban music.

Navigating Controversy and Personal Growth

Throughout his career, YoungBoy’s personal life has been heavily scrutinized by media outlets, legal authorities, and fans alike. Fatherhood has played a central role in his personal narrative; by his mid-twenties, he fathered a large family with multiple partners, a topic he frequently addresses in his deeply confessional lyricism. His brushes with the law, probation terms, and rehabilitation efforts have been chronicled extensively, transforming his public persona into a complex portrait of survival, trauma, and resilience. Despite these hurdles, his music remains an essential cultural touchstone for millions of listeners worldwide.

The Future of an Untamed Hip-Hop Icon

As YoungBoy continues to navigate his twenties and mature as both a father and an artist, his musical direction shows signs of sonic expansion. Experimenting with different subgenres of trap, rock-influenced guitar samples, and deeply personal storytelling, he refuses to be boxed into a single category. Whether performing to sold-out arenas or recording thousands of unreleased tracks from his home studio, his indelible mark on American music ensures that his birth date of October 20, 1999, will forever be remembered as the beginning of a generational hip-hop titan's journey.

Final Thoughts

Key Takeaway: Tracking the birth date and timeline of YoungBoy Never Broke Again reveals an extraordinary trajectory from a troubled Baton Rouge upbringing on October 20, 1999, to becoming one of the most prolific, commercially successful, and streamed independent artists in modern hip-hop history.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact birth date of YoungBoy Never Broke Again?

YoungBoy Never Broke Again was born on October 20, 1999, in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. He grew up in a challenging environment on the city's north side before dropping out of school to pursue music full-time.

When did YoungBoy Never Broke Again release his debut mixtape?

He released his debut independent mixtape titled 'Life Before Fame' in 2015 when he was just fifteen years old. This initial project laid the foundation for his regional fanbase across Louisiana.

How many number-one albums does YoungBoy Never Broke Again have on the Billboard 200?

YoungBoy has achieved multiple number-one albums on the Billboard 200 chart, including 'AI YoungBoy 2', 'Top', and 'Sincerely, Kentrell', making him one of the youngest artists to secure consecutive chart-topping projects.

What was YoungBoy Never Broke Again's birth name?

His birth name is Kentrell DeSean Gaulden. He later adopted the stage name NBA YoungBoy (Never Broke Again) derived from his neighborhood group and his aspirations to achieve financial stability.

Which major record labels has YoungBoy Never Broke Again been signed to?

YoungBoy initially signed a major label deal with Atlantic Records through partnership agreements. After fulfilling those obligations, he transitioned to a major partnership with Motown Records to distribute his extensive musical catalog.
